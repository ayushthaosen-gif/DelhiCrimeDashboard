Road safety, citywide

These figures are published for Delhi as a whole, not per district, so they sit here rather than on the map. District-level counts for 2023 and 2024 (crashes and fatal crashes within the crash-prone zones of each district, crash-prone zones, and persons killed) are in Explore; the district tables for 2016 to 2024 are below. One year, set in the bar under the headline figures, applies to every section.

Sources: Delhi Traffic Police statistics and Delhi Road Crash Reports 2023 and 2024; National Crime Records Bureau for time of day, road type and place, 2014 to 2022. Sources and caveats. Data version 2026-10-20.
