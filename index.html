What to keep in mind

  • Registered cases are not crime levels.Counts depend on reporting and registration as well as on crime.
  • Boundaries changed.Older years use the districts reported at the time, drawn on schematic reference shapes that are labelled inferred or derived.
  • A zero can mean not surveyed.Survey-based layers did not cover every district; OpenStreetMap layers are community-mapped.
  • Crash data uses other districts.Some Traffic Police tables use 11 reporting districts, not the 15 police districts.
