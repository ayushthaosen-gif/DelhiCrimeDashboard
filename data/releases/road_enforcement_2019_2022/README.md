# Enforcement figures, 2019 to 2022

Citywide enforcement figures from the Delhi Traffic Police annual reports, filling the years before the 2023 and 2024 values in [road_safety_extra_2023_2024.json](../../road_safety_extra_2023_2024.json). [JSON](enforcement.json), [CSV](enforcement.csv), [sources, hashes and caveats](manifest.json).

| Measure | 2019 | 2020 | 2021 | 2022 |
|---|---:|---:|---:|---:|
| Hit-and-run fatal crashes | 674 | 579 | 555 | 668 |
| Hit-and-run share of fatal crashes | 47.03% | 49.78% | 46.01% | 46.78% (computed) |
| Drink-driving prosecutions | 36,065 | 3,986 | 2,831 | 2,255 |
| Over-speeding notices (OSVD and tripod cameras) | 25,31,894 | 79,27,515 | 35,29,090 | 28,60,073 |

Camera counts are blank for these years: the reports give inconsistent installation counts (see the manifest). Every row names the report, the PDF page and the file hash; the review status says which figures were checked on the page image. The 2023 and 2024 drink-driving figures are on a basis this repository has not established as the same: read the caveats in the manifest before comparing years.
