# Traffic Police enforcement figures, 2011 to 2018

`enforcement_2011_2018.json` extends the citywide enforcement series on the Road safety page back from 2019 (see `data/releases/road_enforcement_2019_2022/`). Each row names its report, chapter file, table, page, URL, size and SHA-256. The PDFs are not in the repository.

## What is here

| Measure | Years | Source |
|---|---|---|
| Drink-driving prosecutions (persons) | 2011 to 2018 | Table 9.3, DRUNKEN DRIVING, on-the-spot challans |
| Over-speeding challans on the spot | 2011 to 2018 | Table 9.3, OVER SPEED |
| Hit-and-run fatal crashes | 2015 to 2018 | chapter 8 text of each report |
| Hit-and-run share of fatal crashes (%) | 2014 to 2018 | chapter 8 text (2017 computed, see below) |

## How it was read and what was cross-checked

- Table 9.3 was read from the page rendered at 300 dpi. The 2015 report prints 2011 to 2015 and the 2018 report prints 2014 to 2018; the two overlap in 2014 and 2015 and agree for both measures. 2011 to 2013 appear in one report only.
- The 2015 to 2018 drink-driving figures, and the 2017 and 2018 over-speed figures, are also stated in the chapter 8 text of the same reports and agree with the table.
- Hit-and-run counts (623, 647, 700, 765 for 2015 to 2018) are read against the fatal crashes the same reports print (1,582, 1,548, 1,565, 1,657), which equal the citywide series on the site. The 2019 report's own text says "765 in 2018 to 674 in 2019", which joins the 2018 count to the 2019 one already on the site.
- **The 2017 report prints a hit-and-run share of 41.8%, which is its 2016 figure repeated.** 700 of 1,565 fatal crashes is 44.7%, and the same chapter says "around 44.7%". The staged 2017 share is the computed 44.73% and is marked `derived`.

## Do not join across these lines

- **Over-speeding.** Up to 2018 the figure is challans issued on the spot for over-speeding (tens to hundreds of thousands a year). From 2019 the series is notices generated by speed cameras (2.5 million in 2019). They are different things and are held as two measures.
- **Drink-driving.** Persons prosecuted in both periods, but the tables of the 2019 to 2022 reports were not compared line by line with Table 9.3, so read 2018 against 2019 with caution.

## Not held

- Hit-and-run counts for 2011 to 2014: the 2011 to 2014 annual reports are no longer on the Traffic Police site, and the 2015 report gives only the 2014 share (40.6%).
- Camera counts before 2023 (the reports give inconsistent installation counts).
- Helmet, seat-belt and other offence rows of Table 9.3 are printed in the same table and could be added the same way.

Not independently reviewed.
