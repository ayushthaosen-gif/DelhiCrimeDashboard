Reference map, awaiting boundary verification. Station polygons preserved in May 2014 are grouped using the official December 2014 station roster. Their exact effective date and changes between those dates remain unverified. The archived shapes also contain overlaps, including 1.05 km² between reconstructed districts. This map is separate from the dashboard and is not enabled for other years.

Earlier districts cover areas that later became separate districts. For example, the 2014 South-West district includes Dwarka North and South stations; Uttam Nagar is in 2014 West. Later reorganisations must be reconstructed separately.
