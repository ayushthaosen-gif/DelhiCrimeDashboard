Updates

  • Visit of Sh. Taranjit Singh Sandhu, Hon’ble Lieutenant Governor, Delhi
    • Hon’ble Lieutenant Governor, Delhi visited Traffic Headquarters, Todapur.
    • Reviewed on ongoing programs like Traffic Pathshala (to sensitize violators) and Traffic Panchayat (coordinating with civic agencies)
    • Reviewed Traffic Unit operations, enforcement, modernization initiatives, and plans for road decongestion.
    • Reviewed the preparation of upcoming BRICS event.
  • Award Ceremony-Summer Camp-2026
    • Delhi Traffic Police successfully concluded the second batch of the Road Safety Awareness Summer Camp–2026 at four Traffic Training Parks across Delhi.
    • Approximately 750 students participated in the second batch, taking the total participation to nearly 1,390 students across the first two batches.
    • Students engaged in interactive activities including road safety quizzes, traffic drills, painting competitions, and awareness sessions on traffic rules.
    • The camp featured training on first aid, CPR, fire safety, cyber awareness, self-defence, disaster management, and gender sensitization.
    • The third batch is expected to attract around 800 additional students, further strengthening road safety awareness among young road users.
  • Road Safety Pavilion at IITF 2025
    • The Delhi Traffic Police set up an impactful Road Safety Pavilion at the India International Trade Fair 2025, receiving strong appreciation from visitors of all age groups.
    • Through creative displays, live demonstrations and interactive activities, the pavilion highlighted the importance of safe and disciplined road behaviour.
    • Educational games, quizzes, drawing contests, a Riding Trainer Simulator and CPR training offered practical learning experiences.
    • Nukkad Nataks, road safety literature, a Selfie Point and a Pledge Board further enhanced public engagement.
    • Over 2,49,000 visitors, including 5,000 students, were sensitized about road safety, reaffirming the Delhi Traffic Police’s commitment to building a safer Delhi.
