# Draft: Right to Information request for crash-zone geotags (Delhi Road Crash Report 2024)

**This is a draft for the project owner to review, adapt and file. Nothing has been sent.**
Check which Public Information Officer (PIO) and portal apply before filing (the Delhi Police / Traffic Police PIO; the central
RTI portal at https://rtionline.gov.in is commonly used for Delhi Police, but confirm), and add your name, address and fee details.

---

**To:** The Public Information Officer, Traffic Unit / Delhi Police (Delhi Traffic Police), New Delhi

**Subject:** Request for information under Section 6(1) of the Right to Information Act, 2005: geotag coordinates of crash-prone zones in the "Delhi Road Crash Report 2024"

**Information requested**

The Delhi Road Crash Report 2024, published by Delhi Traffic Police, lists crash-prone locations (for example Table 6.29, and the
pedestrian, two-wheeler, heavy-vehicle, hit-and-run, day-time, night-time and CCTV-priority tables) and states that crash spots were
geotagged. The report does not publish coordinates. Please provide, in electronic form (CSV or spreadsheet):

1. For every crash-prone location / zone named in the report (111 zones as stated in the report), the **latitude and longitude** used by
   Delhi Traffic Police, the **geotag method** (for example crash-record geotags, a representative point, or a centroid), and the **radius or extent**
   of the zone if one was used.
2. The **methodology** used to identify and rank these zones (the search radius, the minimum number of crashes, the period and the data source).
3. **Anonymised crash-level records for calendar year 2024** for Delhi, with only these fields: date, time of day, latitude, longitude, police
   station area, road name, vehicle types involved, victim category (for example pedestrian, two-wheeler rider, cyclist), and severity
   (fatal, grievous, minor, no injury). **No names, addresses, phone numbers, registration numbers or other personal identifiers are requested.**
4. If item 3 cannot be given in full, the same fields **aggregated to the zones listed in the report**, or a **list of location descriptions
   precise enough to place each zone** (nearest junction, road section or chainage, police station).

**Purpose (optional to state):** independent public-interest analysis of road safety and infrastructure coverage in Delhi, published as an open
dashboard. The coordinates in the public version are currently approximate because the report does not publish them.

**Format:** electronic (CSV/XLSX), by email or portal download, as allowed under Section 7(9).

**Fee:** [attach the prescribed fee or state BPL status with proof].

**Applicant:** [name], [address], [phone], [email]

**Date:** [date]

---

## Notes for the applicant
- Items 1 and 2 are the most important. Item 3 is a larger ask and may be refused in part; item 4 is the fallback.
- If personal-information exemptions are cited, point out that no personal identifiers are requested.
- If the answer is "not available", ask for the PIO's reasons in writing and consider a first appeal to the First Appellate Authority.
- An iRAD/eDAR route also exists: ask the same unit whether iRAD can export 2024 crash coordinates.
