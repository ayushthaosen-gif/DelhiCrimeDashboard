# Draft: RTI request to DDA for the existing land-use survey (GIS)

**This is a draft for the project owner to review, adapt and file. Nothing has been sent.**
Check the right Public Information Officer (PIO) and portal before filing (DDA's GIS / Planning wing; DDA has an RTI cell), and add your details and fee.

---

**To:** The Public Information Officer, Delhi Development Authority (Planning / GIS Unit), Vikas Sadan, INA, New Delhi

**Subject:** Request for information under Section 6(1) of the Right to Information Act, 2005: existing land-use data used as the base for the Master Plan for Delhi

**Information requested**

1. The **existing land-use survey / base map** used for the Master Plan for Delhi 2047 (and the 2021 or 2041 base survey if that is what the plan uses), as GIS data
   (shapefile, GeoJSON or geodatabase) with the land-use category field, survey year and coordinate system. If it exists only as maps, the maps at the highest resolution available.
2. The **land-use legend and category definitions** used in that survey, and the survey **methodology** (source imagery, field verification, minimum mapping unit).
3. The **existing land use by MCD ward**, or by planning zone / planning unit, as a table (area in hectares by category), if the GIS data cannot be shared.
4. Whether the existing-land-use layers are published anywhere (portal, URL), and if not, the reason they are not.

**Purpose (optional to state):** public-interest comparison of the Master Plan's planned land use with existing land use, published as an open dashboard.

**Format:** electronic, by email or portal download (Section 7(9)).  **Fee:** [attach fee or BPL proof].  **Applicant:** [name, address, phone, email]  **Date:** [date]

---

## Notes
- Item 1 is the main ask; item 3 is a fallback that is much easier for them to provide.
- DDA already publishes the *planned* (2047) land use and several settlement layers (Lal Dora, PM-UDAY colonies, JJ clusters) on its GIS server; this request is about the *existing* use.
- If refused under Section 8, ask for the exemption relied on in writing and consider a first appeal.
