# Draft: RTI request to the Municipal Corporation of Delhi for property counts by ward and use

**This is a draft for the project owner to review, adapt and file. Nothing has been sent.**
Check the right PIO (MCD Property Tax Department / Headquarters; the corporation's RTI portal) and add your details and fee.

---

**To:** The Public Information Officer, Municipal Corporation of Delhi (Property Tax Department), Civic Centre, New Delhi

**Subject:** Request for information under Section 6(1) of the Right to Information Act, 2005: number and area of assessed properties by ward and use category

**Information requested**

For each of the 250 MCD wards (using the ward names or numbers in the current delimitation), for the latest assessment year available:

1. The **number of assessed properties** and the **total covered area (sq m)** by use category: residential, commercial, mixed use, industrial, and other categories used in the property tax system (for example educational, institutional, vacant land).
2. The **colony category (A to H)** assigned to each ward, or the list of colonies in each ward with their category, as used for the unit area value.
3. The **definitions** of the use categories used for property tax assessment, and the date of the data.
4. If ward-wise data cannot be provided, the same figures by **MCD zone**.

**No individual property, owner or tax payer details are requested**: only counts and totals aggregated by ward and category.

**Purpose (optional to state):** public-interest comparison of land use with the Master Plan, published as an open dashboard.

**Format:** electronic (CSV or spreadsheet), by email or portal download.  **Fee:** [attach fee or BPL proof].  **Applicant:** [name, address, phone, email]  **Date:** [date]

---

## Notes
- Stress that only aggregates are requested; this avoids personal-information exemptions.
- Ward delimitation changed in 2022 (250 wards). The existing dashboard ward layer is the older 290-feature grid, so ask for the ward list used in the response and join by name.
