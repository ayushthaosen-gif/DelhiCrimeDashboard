District iRAD reports prototype

Road-accident counts from the Integrated Road Accident Database reports that Delhi's district administrations post, staged for review in data/derived/irad_monthly/. Monthly tables by district and police-station tables where published.

Read before comparing. iRAD counts come from records with minimal information and are a different series from the Delhi Traffic Police Road Crash Report used elsewhere on this site. This page shows only what has been loaded here, not everything published: the district sites list 113 further reports (East, North East, South, South West, Dwarka and New Delhi station tables) that are not loaded yet. Coverage also differs by district (see below), the earlier months of a year can be undercounts, and several revenue districts are not comparable with the 15 police districts. Rows marked scan were read from scanned PDFs by Claude and need a human spot-check.
