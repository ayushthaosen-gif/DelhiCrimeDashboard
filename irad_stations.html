District iRAD reports prototype

Road-accident counts from the Integrated Road Accident Database reports that Delhi's district administrations post, staged for review in data/derived/irad_monthly/. Monthly tables by district and police-station tables where published.

Read before comparing. iRAD counts come from records with minimal information and are a different series from the Delhi Traffic Police Road Crash Report used elsewhere on this site. Several revenue districts are not comparable with the 15 police districts, and the earlier months of a year can be undercounts.
