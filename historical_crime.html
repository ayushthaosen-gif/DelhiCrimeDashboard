Derived reference map. The official 9 November 2017 Delhi Gazette schedule (14 districts, 163 territorial stations) is applied to the May 2014 station shapes. The outlines are schematic and not to scale: they are lightly smoothed for display, so they are not usable for measuring area or placing a point.
