Planning mode

For urban planners, journalists and researchers: see the crash and infrastructure context the data holds for a ward, with its year, source, coverage and uncertainty stated beside it, then take an evidence pack to a field visit.

Crash layers show the locations the Traffic Police list as crash-prone, placed approximately; infrastructure layers are what volunteers have mapped in OpenStreetMap. Nothing here says what caused a crash or what should be built.
